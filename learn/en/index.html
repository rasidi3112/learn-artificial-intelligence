An open course in artificial intelligence

Understand how machines learn, from first principles to real systems.

Fifteen concise modules, from the history of AI and machine learning to robotics, edge AI and governance. Read at your own pace, test what you know, and keep notes. Everything stays on your device.

All modules

  1. 01 What is Artificial Intelligence? Learn the basic concepts of AI, its history, and applications in daily life. Beginner · 15 min
  2. 02 Machine Learning Basics Understand Machine Learning concepts, supervised and unsupervised learning. Intermediate · 20 min
  3. 03 Deep Learning & Neural Networks Introduction to deep learning and how neural networks work. Advanced · 25 min
  4. 04 Natural Language Processing (NLP) How AI understands and processes human language. Intermediate · 18 min
  5. 05 Computer Vision AI technology for recognizing and processing images and videos. Advanced · 22 min
  6. 06 Basic Prompt Engineering The art of interacting with generative AI like ChatGPT for maximum results. Beginner · 15 min
  7. 07 Ethics and Bias in AI Discussing the importance of security, privacy, and fairness in using AI. Intermediate · 20 min
  8. 08 Career Guide in AI Job opportunities, future trends, and how to start a career in the AI industry. Beginner · 15 min
  9. 09 Reinforcement Learning Learn how AI agents make decisions through rewards, simulation, and policy evaluation. Advanced · 28 min
  10. 10 Generative AI & Large Language Models Understand models that generate text, images, and code, and use them with controlled data. Intermediate · 26 min
  11. 11 AI in Healthcare Explore medical AI, clinical validation, and safety limits before use in healthcare. Intermediate · 25 min
  12. 12 AI for Business & Industry Connect business problems with data, models, operational metrics, and accountable governance. Intermediate · 24 min
  13. 13 Robotics & AI Learn how perception, planning, control, and learning enable robots to work in the physical world. Advanced · 30 min
  14. 14 Edge AI & IoT Run AI inference close to sensors within device limits for power, memory, connectivity, and security. Advanced · 27 min
  15. 15 AutoML & No-Code AI Use automation to build models while still testing data, metrics, and resulting decisions. Beginner · 20 min
