Career Guide in AI

Job opportunities, future trends, and how to start a career in the AI industry.

Level: Beginner Duration: 15 min

01 Career Paths in AI

  1. Machine Learning Engineer: Building and deploying AI models to production.
  2. Data Scientist: Analyzing complex data for business insights.
  3. Data Engineer: Building infrastructure and pipelines for data processing.
  4. AI Researcher: Conducting research to discover new algorithm breakthroughs.
  5. Prompt Engineer: Testing and maximizing Generative AI outputs.

02 Essential Skills

  • Programming Languages: Python, R, SQL.
  • Mathematics: Linear Algebra, Calculus, Probability, and Statistics.
  • Tools/Frameworks: PyTorch, TensorFlow, Scikit-learn, Pandas.
  • Soft Skills: Problem solving, communication, and critical thinking.

03 How to Get Started

Start by strengthening your Python and math foundations. Take online courses on Machine Learning basics (like the modules on this platform!), try building small portfolio projects (like simple image classification or price prediction), then participate in communities like Kaggle or GitHub.
